WHO WE ARE

We believe every young person deserves the chance to become more.

ShiftQuest Africa is a youth-focused initiative committed to unlocking potential, nurturing leadership and creating pathways for meaningful contribution.

We bring people, ideas and opportunities together through mentorship, leadership development, entrepreneurship, innovation and community engagement. Our work looks beyond titles and qualifications to the person, purpose and possibilities behind them.
